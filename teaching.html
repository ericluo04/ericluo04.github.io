Research opportunities

I work with students who want hands-on experience with unstructured data (images, text, video), generative models, and causal inference in marketing settings. Prior coursework or research experience in machine learning, econometrics, or statistics is required, along with experience using Claude Code or Codex and familiarity with Python or R.

If you are a Yale student interested in a research assistantship or an independent study, email me at lan.luo@yale.edu with a short note on what you have worked on, what you would like to learn, and your CV or transcript. If you are a prospective Ph.D. applicant, please apply through the Yale SOM doctoral program rather than contacting faculty individually. The admissions committee reads every file.
